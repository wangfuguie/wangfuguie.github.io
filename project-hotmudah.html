專案介紹Overview

HotMudah AI 是一個上線營運中的訂閱制平台，讓沒有設計與行銷人力的商家也能自己做廣告。商家先設定一次品牌與產品，之後每次只要用一句話說需求；AI 會先用問卷問清楚、寫出企劃或分鏡，等使用者確認後才開始生成，所以每次的成品都符合同一套品牌規則。平台能做廣告圖片、可逐段編輯分鏡的廣告短片，以及 25 款樣板的產品銷售網頁，並內建訂閱方案、點數計費、團隊席位與管理後台，金流串接 Stripe 與馬來西亞的 Fiuu。

HotMudah AI is a live subscription platform that lets businesses without design or marketing staff make their own ads. A business sets up its brand and products once and then describes each need in a sentence; the AI asks clarifying questions and drafts a plan or storyboard first, generating only after the user approves, so every output follows the same brand rules. It produces ad images, short ad videos with an editable storyboard, and product landing pages from 25 templates, with subscriptions, credit billing, team seats and an admin console built in and payments through Stripe and Malaysia’s Fiuu.

我負責的部分My role

平台要正式上線收費，必須在許多商家同時使用、而且每次 AI 生成都要等一段時間的情況下保持穩定。我是這個專案的主要開發者，從後端、前端到 AWS 部署都由我負責，並且要確認正式環境撐得住 100 人同時使用。

The platform was going live as a paid service and had to stay stable with many businesses using it at once, each waiting on slow AI generation. I was the lead developer, responsible for the backend, frontend and AWS deployment, and for proving the live environment could handle 100 concurrent users.

具體貢獻What I did

  1. 全端開發與雲端部署Full-stack development and deployment

    完成平台的後端、前端與 AWS 部署；專案 295 筆程式提交中，有 249 筆由我完成。

    Built the backend and frontend and deployed the platform on AWS, authoring 249 of the project’s 295 commits.

  2. 正式環境壓力測試Load testing in production

    在 AWS 正式環境模擬 100 人同時操作，一般操作的 7,435 次請求全數成功。

    Simulated 100 concurrent users on the live AWS environment; all 7,435 general requests succeeded.

  3. 找出並修正效能瓶頸Finding and fixing a bottleneck

    從測試結果發現系統在等 AI 回應時會一直佔住資料庫連線，修正後回應時間（p95）從 618 毫秒降到 42 毫秒。

    Traced slow responses to database connections being held while waiting on AI, and cut p95 latency from 618 ms to 42 ms.

專案介紹Overview

這是給企業資安團隊用的「資安情報儀表板」。系統自動收集外部的資安新聞與最新漏洞，把原本分散在各處的情資集中成三類：資安新聞、軟體漏洞（CVE）、可疑 IP／網址／檔案查詢（IoC）。首頁用圖表顯示漏洞的風險等級分布，點一下就能看明細；使用者訂閱關鍵字後，系統會依設定的時間把相關新聞與漏洞整理成信寄出，還會比對公司資產的設備品牌，主動提醒「和我們有關」的資安事件。

A security-intelligence dashboard for an enterprise security team. It automatically collects public security news and new vulnerabilities, bringing scattered intelligence together in three types: security news, software vulnerabilities (CVE) and lookups for suspicious IPs, URLs and files (IoC). The home page charts vulnerabilities by risk level with one click through to details; after subscribing to keywords, users get scheduled email digests, and incidents are matched against the company’s equipment brands to flag what is actually relevant.

我負責的部分My role

資安情資分散在新聞網站、漏洞資料庫等不同地方，資安監控中心團隊要一個一個去查，也不容易看出哪些和公司的設備有關。我是 3 人團隊中的開發人員，負責系統架構、登入驗證，以及情資蒐集與提醒功能。

Security intelligence was scattered across news sites and vulnerability databases; the Security Operations Center team had to check each one and could not easily tell what affected the company’s equipment. As a developer on a three-person team, I owned the architecture, sign-in, and the intelligence collection and alerting features.

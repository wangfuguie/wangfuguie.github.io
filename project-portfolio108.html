專案介紹Overview

「108 換個方式」是一個幫高中生寫學習歷程的網站。輔助撰寫功能讓學生選好想申請的校系與文件類型（共四種），再用一題一題的提問引導他們回想做過什麼、學到什麼，最後由 ChatGPT 依目標科系整理成通順、有個人特色的文章。學生也可以貼上自己寫的內容做文字美化，AI 會保留原本的文風並強化與目標學系的關聯；另外還能從 18 學群逐層查看學系介紹，用互動圖表了解每個學類需要的能力與特質。

“108 Another Way” is a website that helps high-school students write their learning portfolio for university applications. In guided writing, students pick a target department and one of four document types, answer step-by-step questions about what they did and learned, and ChatGPT turns the answers into a polished, personal essay tailored to that department. They can also paste their own draft for polishing, which keeps their voice while strengthening the link to the target department, and browse from 18 academic clusters down to departments with an interactive chart of the abilities each field looks for.

我負責的部分My role

企業想用大語言模型協助高中生撰寫學習歷程，但一開始需求與範圍都還不明確。我擔任 10 人團隊的專案經理，負責釐清需求、界定範疇並控管進度。

The company wanted to use large language models to help high-school students write their portfolios, but requirements and scope were unclear at the start. As project manager of a ten-person team, I was responsible for clarifying requirements, defining scope and controlling progress.

具體貢獻What I did

  1. 需求訪談與專案章程Requirements and project charter

    和企業進行需求訪談、確認專案目標，撰寫專案章程並界定範疇。

    Ran requirement interviews with the company, confirmed goals, wrote the project charter and defined scope.

  2. 收斂專案範圍Cutting scope

    第一版規劃過大時，帶團隊收斂成做得出來的第二版，聚焦學生端三大核心功能：輔助撰寫、文字美化、學類學系探索。

    When the first plan proved too ambitious, led the team to a buildable second version focused on three student features: guided writing, text polishing and the department explorer.

  3. 進度控管與分工Schedule control and responsibilities

    用 WBS 拆解開發流程、制定 ITTO 規則控管進度，並以 RACI 矩陣分配團隊職責。

    Broke the work down with a WBS, controlled execution with ITTO rules and assigned responsibilities with a RACI matrix.

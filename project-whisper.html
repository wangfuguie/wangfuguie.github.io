LLM 多模態輿情分析功能開發LLM-Powered Broadcast News Monitoring

一套 24 小時自動「聽」電視新聞頻道的系統：把直播的聲音即時轉成文字，再用 AI 修正錯字、去掉重複，並分出哪些是新聞、哪些是廣告，整理好存進資料庫。

A system that “listens” to a TV news channel around the clock: it turns live audio into text in near real time, then uses AI to fix errors, remove duplicates and separate news from ads before storing the result.

專案介紹Overview

這套系統 24 小時自動「聽」電視新聞頻道，取代人工守著電視做逐字稿。直播、每日固定新聞時段與每週節目三種來源都會全自動處理：先把聲音即時轉成文字，再用三個階段的 AI 處理修正錯字、補上標點、去掉重複，並貼上【新聞】或【其他】標籤把廣告和串場內容濾掉，最後整理成可以直接分析的文章存進資料庫。AI 模型可以抽換，雲端的 Gemini 或在自己電腦上跑的開源模型都能用。

This system listens to a TV news channel around the clock, replacing people who watch TV to produce transcripts. Live broadcast, daily news slots and weekly programs are all handled automatically: audio becomes text in near real time, then a three-stage AI process fixes errors, adds punctuation, removes duplicates and tags each passage as news or other to filter out ads and filler, storing clean, analysis-ready articles. The AI model is swappable between cloud-based Gemini and open-source models running locally.

我負責的部分My role

要掌握電視新聞內容，原本得有人守著電視做逐字稿；而語音辨識直接輸出的文字有錯字、沒標點又重複，沒辦法直接拿來分析。這套系統由我一個人獨立開發，從擷取直播聲音、轉成文字，到整理成乾淨的文章存進資料庫。

Keeping up with TV news meant someone watching and transcribing, and raw speech-to-text was full of errors, unpunctuated and repetitive. I built this system alone, from capturing the live audio and transcribing it to producing clean articles in a database.

具體貢獻What I did

  1. 直播音訊擷取Live audio capture

    用爬蟲即時監聽新聞直播，擷取音訊封包並轉檔。

    Wrote a crawler that monitors the live stream, captures audio packets and converts them.

  2. 語音辨識方案選型Choosing the speech-recognition engine

    比較多個語音辨識方案後，選用硬體需求較低的 whisper.cpp 做即時轉錄。

    Compared several options and chose whisper.cpp for real-time transcription on modest hardware.

  3. 三階段 AI 文字清理Three-stage AI text cleanup

    設計分段貼標、深度清理、去除重複三個階段的處理流程，並比較三種語言模型的表現。

    Designed the three-stage process (segment and tag, deep clean, de-duplicate) and compared three language models.

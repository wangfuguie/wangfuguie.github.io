專案介紹Overview

這是一個會自動讀新聞的 LINE 機器人，幫政府單位掌握輿情。系統用同一套標準自動判斷約 4.4 萬則新聞是正面、負面還是中立，每天找出「今天才出現」的新事件，並預測話題接下來兩天會不會繼續延燒。使用者在 LINE 訂閱關心的主題，就會收到新聞數量、正負面比例與走勢預測的圖表；機器人另外提供即時新聞、即時分析、訂閱管理、主題推播、歷史資料與今日熱門事件六項功能。

A LINE chatbot that reads the news to help government agencies track public opinion. It labels about 44,000 articles as positive, negative or neutral by one consistent standard, finds events that first appeared today, and forecasts whether a topic will keep heating up over the next two days. Users subscribe to topics in LINE and receive charts of news volume, sentiment mix and the forecast; the bot also offers six features: latest news, instant analysis, subscriptions, scheduled pushes, history and today’s hot events.

我負責的部分My role

政府單位要掌握輿情，原本得靠人工一篇一篇讀新聞，量大、標準不容易一致，也很難提早發現新事件。我擔任 6 人團隊的組長，在 36 週內帶團隊做出能判斷正負面、找出新事件並預測趨勢的系統。

Agencies tracking public opinion relied on people reading articles one by one: high volume, inconsistent standards and little chance of spotting new events early. As lead of a six-person team, I delivered a system in 36 weeks that labels sentiment, detects new events and forecasts trends.

具體貢獻What I did

  1. 專案管理與架構設計Project management and architecture

    負責 36 週的專案管理，並設計整體功能架構。

    Ran project management over 36 weeks and designed the overall architecture.

  2. 正負面判斷的提示詞設計Prompt design for sentiment

    設計讓 Claude 判斷新聞正負面的提示詞，包含角色設定、逐步推理、範例與輸出格式限制，用來自動標記約 4.4 萬則新聞。

    Designed the prompt Claude used to judge sentiment (role, step-by-step reasoning, an example, strict output format), which labeled about 44,000 articles automatically.

  3. 事件分群與趨勢預測模型Event clustering and forecasting

    訓練 HDBSCAN 事件分群與 Prophet 預測模型，完成「今天才出現的新事件」洞察與未來兩天的發酵趨勢預測。

    Built the HDBSCAN event clustering and Prophet forecasting behind new-event detection and the two-day trend forecast.

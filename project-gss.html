專案介紹Overview

這是一個讀過 Gogoro 車主手冊的 AI 客服，是我們在「雲湧智生：臺灣生成式 AI 應用黑客松」針對 Gogoro 出的題目做出的作品。車主先選自己的車款，再用中文或英文發問；它只在那一款車的手冊裡找答案，避免把別款車的內容答給車主，回答時還會附上手冊裡的圖片。問題不清楚時它會先反問，和 Gogoro 無關的問題不回答，手冊沒寫的就直接說沒有，不會亂編。

An AI assistant that has read the Gogoro owner’s manuals, built for Gogoro’s challenge at the Taiwan Generative AI Applications Hackathon. Riders pick their scooter model and ask in Chinese or English; it searches only that model’s manual, so nobody gets another scooter’s instructions, and answers include the manual’s pictures. It asks back when a question is unclear, declines off-topic questions, and says so when the manual has no answer instead of making one up.

我負責的部分My role

這是黑客松上 Gogoro 出的題目，團隊要在活動期間做出能回答車主問題的 AI 客服，並向評審說明。我擔任隊長，帶隊完成作品與上台報告；個人主要負責提示詞設計、測試與簡報，程式開發由隊友負責。

Gogoro set this hackathon challenge: build an AI assistant that answers riders’ questions within the event and pitch it to the judges. As team captain I led the team to a finished product and the final pitch; my own work was prompt design, testing and the presentation, while teammates wrote the code.

  1. 帶隊與成果報告Leading the team and the pitch

    帶領團隊在黑客松期間完成作品，並上台向評審報告。

    Led the team to complete the product during the hackathon and delivered the final pitch.

  2. 三組提示詞設計Three sets of prompts

    分別設計對話情境與車款設定、AI 如何呼叫查詢工具、如何根據查到的內容作答。

    Designed prompts for conversation context and model selection, for how the AI calls the search tool, and for answering from retrieved content.

  3. 測試與技術簡報Testing and technical slides

    負責測試，並把技術成果整理成評審一看就懂的架構圖與簡報。

    Ran testing and turned the technical work into diagrams and slides the judges could grasp at a glance.
